Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Desktop Web Widget does not collect, store, sell, or transmit personal data to the app developer.

Local Data

The app stores widget layout data locally on your device, including widget positions, sizes, URLs, titles, opacity, layers, and presets. This data is used only to restore your layout.

Websites You Load

The app lets you enter web page URLs and display those pages inside desktop widgets. Websites loaded by you may have their own privacy policies, cookies, analytics, login systems, or tracking behavior. Those third-party websites are controlled by their respective owners.

No Analytics

The app does not include developer-operated analytics, advertising, crash reporting, user accounts, or payment tracking.
