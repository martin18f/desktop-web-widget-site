FAQ

Before you buy.

Does the app require Node.js or coding knowledge?

No. The download includes a normal Windows installer. You do not need Node.js, npm, Electron, or programming knowledge.

Can every website be displayed?

The app uses isolated browser views, so normal websites such as ChatGPT can load even when they reject iframe embedding. Some DRM services, enterprise login policies, extension-dependent pages, or sites that explicitly reject desktop app browsers may still be unavailable.

Can I click inside widgets?

Yes. Widgets are clickable in edit mode. You can also choose whether each widget remains interactive after locking the layout.

Is this a subscription?

No. Desktop Web Widget is sold as a one-time purchase. QR payment, Stripe Checkout, and Gumroad are available as purchase options.
